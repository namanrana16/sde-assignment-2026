# Monte Carlo of the experiment decision rule (C6) and decay response (C7)

Generated by `python scripts/mc_null.py --runs 800 --power-runs 300 --decay-runs 250` (the script's defaults are 1000 / 400 / 300); applies `controller.exp_rules`, `exp_alloc` and `exp_decay` unchanged at 4-hour tick resolution (looks at each day's end).

Setup: 3000 dials/day at pickup 0.3 (about 900 connects/day), engagement 0.5, 4500 leads with intra-lead correlation 0.1; one control and two (ramp mix: three) challengers entering at 7.5% (ramp to 20% after >= 1 day and >= 100 connects at point >= control; drop on the whole-experiment z <= -1.5 below 300 connects, <= -1 above); one workflow test (alpha 0.020000000000000004) and two half-traffic analysis groups (alpha 0.0025000000000000005 each, 0.025 per dim). Contrasts are Mantel-Haenszel pooled over strata of day x share regime. Winner: <= 4 Lan-DeMets OBF looks at information 0.25/0.5/0.75/1, Holm, point lift >= 3%, recent-24h one-sided 95% lower bound > 0, pickup non-inferiority. Promotion is two-stage: stage 1 winner 50% / old control 50%; stage 2 90/10 after >= 24 h at stage 1 with a positive recent lower bound and concurrent contrast. Every approved change waits an approval latency, then is re-checked (promotion stages: recent-window one-sided 80% lower bound > 0; ramp: z and recent z; drop: still at the drop bound) and applied or withheld. Decay: sequential winner-vs-control test (alpha 0.1 per epoch, Lan-DeMets OBF, epoch = information for a -15% effect at 80% power) plus CUSUM confirmed by the holdout direction. The harm-stop (removal only) is omitted, which is conservative.

False-winner rate under the null (any arm, any scope): **1.0%** (target <= 5%). A null scenario has 4 experiments (2 workflows x 2 dims), so the chance of at least one false promotion is about 4x this. The unstratified drift row is the comparison: at a +-5% drift the two false-winner rates are indistinguishable (the drift averages out over day-end looks and the shares move little before a winner is declared), so stratification costs no size or power here and only guards the contrast against confounding when shares and the base rate move together.
